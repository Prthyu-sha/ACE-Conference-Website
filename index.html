About

ACE Engineering College

ACE Engineering College in Ankushapur, Ghatkesar, Hyderabad, Telangana, is a premier engineering institution established in 2007 by the Yadala Satyanarayana Memorial Educational Society. The college is affiliated with Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.
The Institution offers undergraduate programs in major engineering disciplines such as Civil Engineering, Mechnical Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Electronics and communication Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Data Science, IOT, and Information Technology. Several programs are NBA accredited, reflecting the institute's commitment to academic quality and continuous improvement.Spread across a 10-acre campus in the suburbs of Hyderabad, the collge provides modern laboratories, research, innovation, and a condutive learning environment for students. The institution follows Outcome-Based Education (OBE) and emphasizes research, innovation, and industry collaboration to prepare students for global technological challenges.With a strong focus on academic excellence, professional ethics, and societal responsibility, ACE Engineering College aims to nurture competent engineers capable of contributing effectively to technological advancement and national development.

About the Conference

The International Conference on Trends in Quantum Compution and Emerging Technology (ICTQCET-2026) is being organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at ACE Engineering College, Hyderabad. The conference aims to provide an international platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students to share their research findings and discuss recent advancements in the fields of quantum computing and emerging technologies. Quantum computing has the potential to revolutionize modern computing by solving complex problems that are beyond the capabilities of classical computers. Along with rapidly evolving technologies such as Artificial Intelligence , Machine Learning, Data Science, Internet of Things (IOT), Cloud Computing, and Cyber Security, these developments are shaping the future of engineering and technology. ICTQCET-2026 seeks to promote innovative research, interdisciplinary collaboration, and knowledge exchange among participants from academia, research organizatons, and industry across the world. The conference will feature keynote addresses by eminent experts, technical paper presentations, and interactive discussions, providing a valuable opportunity for networking and collaboration.
The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at ACE Engineering college is committed to excellence in teaching, research and innovation in the field of computer science. The department offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs designed to equip students with strrong theoretical knowledge and practical skills in emerging technologies. The department has well-equipped laboratories, experienced faculty members, and an academic environment that encourages research, innovation, and industry collaboration. It actively promotes student participation in technical activities, workshops, hackathons, and research projects in areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, and Internet of Things(IoT). The conference also focuses on mutually sharing the advances and innovative technologies

Call for papers

• Prospective authors are invited to submit their high quality, original, unpublished research paper (Full Paper maximum 8 pages and beyond 8 pages chargers are applicable per page), the abstract should be of 200 to 250 words in 12 pt Italic Times New Roman with minimum four to five key words. Manuscript should be typed in 12 font size, Times New Roman, single spaced with 1" margin on standard A4 size paper.

• Submitted papers must be written in English only and provide sufficient details to allow the conference Technical Program Committee (TPC) to assess and evaluate the merits in view of technical quality and relevance to the conference theme.

• All the papers must be submitted on or before the deadline only.

• All the submissions will go through the plagiarism check and double blind peer review by at least two reviewers.
